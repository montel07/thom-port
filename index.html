About Me

Introduction

Hello! I'm Thomas, and i am from Nigeria. Yeah, you might be wondering why Montel was left out. Well, that is because it isn't my real name, it is a moniker of mine. A popular one, i might add. Well, as for what i do, i have worked on a few video editing projects over the pass 3 years, and i have been ghostwriting for a year now. Having over 50 different clients with dissimilar interests. I’ve always been drawn to understanding how things work and finding ways to merge creativity with functionality whether through coding, exploring new art forms, or immersing myself in stories through books and games.

Hobbies

Books have always been a constant in my life. I enjoy reading across genres, from classic literature to science fiction, and even non-fiction on topics like psychology and technology. I believe books offer unique perspectives and insights that help shape not just knowledge but also resourcefulness and creativity. I’m also a huge fan of gaming technology. I’m fascinated by how games combine complex coding, immersive storytelling, and stunning visual design to create interactive worlds. Gaming isn’t just a pastime for me; it’s an inspiration for how technology and art can blend seamlessly to create memorable experiences. It was this passion that actually led me to explore 3D art, where i only made two videos off tutorials on YouTube.

Jobs

On the professional side, I landed a job where I managed and moderate online posts for a company. It’s a role that gave me a new appreciation for social media and the importance of curating content that connects and resonates with people. I learned a lot about engaging with diverse audiences and maintaining an online presence that’s both professional and relatable. That experience helped me build communication skills that I know will be valuable, whether in tech or any other field. But now, i work as a freelance video editor and writer.

Goal

I’m currently learning 3D design using Blender, an incredible tool for modeling, animation, and rendering. It’s a challenging journey, and I’d be lying if I said I was a pro at it yet. Progress has been slow as I work to understand everything from basic shapes to complex textures and lighting. Every small step feels rewarding, though, and I love seeing the ideas gradually come to life, (Credits: YouTube - Andrew Price) no matter how rough the final product may look at this stage. In the future, I hope to bridge my interests in technology, art, and communication. I want to create things that inspire and engage, whether it’s through writing, game development, editing, or 3D design. I believe technology is a powerful tool for innovation and storytelling, and I’m excited to be part of a field where the possibilities are endless.
